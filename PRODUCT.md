## Design Context

### Users
This portfolio is for service-based business owners who need web and digital solutions from a freelance front-end designer. They are likely evaluating taste, clarity, trust, and whether the designer can turn a business offer into a polished digital presence that converts.

### Brand Personality
Strategic, intentional, refined, human, clear, and conversion-focused. The experience should feel bold and curiosity-provoking without becoming cluttered or overly decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction
Minimal editorial structure inspired by Spencer Gabor's sparse portfolio rhythm: direct identity, strong work presentation, and confident spacing. The palette draws from black, warm off-white, coral, peach, and muted sage: #111111, #FAF6F2, #FF7A59, #FFDCC8, #A6B8A0. Motion should feel deliberate and tactile, with GSAP-driven reveals and a restrained Three.js or React Three Fiber element that makes the site memorable.

### Design Principles
- Lead with a bold statement and immediate clarity about the offer.
- Use motion to reveal intent, not to decorate everything.
- Make the work section the most dynamic part of the site.
- Keep the visual system simple enough to feel refined and robust.
- Balance curiosity with trust so business owners feel both intrigued and confident.
